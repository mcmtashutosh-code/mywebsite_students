ध्येयोदय (Dhyeodaya) कोचिंग संस्थानों के लिए ऑनलाइन टेस्ट प्लेटफ़ॉर्म है। छात्र का नाम, मोबाइल, ईमेल, टेस्ट के उत्तर और परिणाम केवल आपके अपने संस्थान को पढ़ाई और परीक्षा के लिए दिखते हैं। हम आपका डेटा बेचते नहीं, विज्ञापन नहीं दिखाते, और दूसरे संस्थान आपका डेटा नहीं देख सकते। पासवर्ड एन्क्रिप्ट (hash) करके रखा जाता है। अपना डेटा देखने, सुधारने या मिटाने के लिए पहले अपने संस्थान से, या नीचे दिए ईमेल पर हमसे संपर्क करें।

1. Who we are

Dhyeodaya (“we”, “us”) is an online examination and practice platform for coaching institutes, operated by Ashutosh Kumar Dubey, India. Each institute (“Institute”) uses Dhyeodaya under its own name to run tests for its own students.

  • Student data: the Institute decides why and how its students’ data is used, so the Institute is the Data Fiduciary. We process that data on the Institute’s behalf as its Data Processor, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).
  • Institute account and payment data, and visitors to dhyeodaya.in: we are the Data Fiduciary.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your Institute: its admins see their own students’ data only. One Institute can never see another Institute’s students.
  • Other students of your Institute: may see your name and points on challenge leaderboards and milestone banners. Your test marks and rank are shown only to you (the Institute may share a merit list; it shows only the last digits of mobile numbers). Your mobile number and email are never shown to other students.
  • Service providers that host or deliver the service for us, only to the extent needed: Supabase (database and storage), GitHub Pages (hosting of the app pages), Google (sending emails through Gmail; web fonts). Some of these providers may store or process data outside India.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

4. How we protect it

  • Passwords and PINs are stored as one-way (bcrypt) hashes; nobody, including us, can read them.
  • The apps cannot read the database directly; every action goes through checked server functions, and each Institute’s data is separated.
  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a short time.
  • Payment records cannot be deleted silently; changes are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

5. How long we keep it

  • Student and Institute data is kept while the Institute’s subscription is active.
  • After a subscription ends, data is kept for a limited period so the Institute can export it, and then deleted, except records the law requires us to keep (for example payment and tax records, and logs the law requires to be kept for at least one year).
  • Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

6. Children

Many students are below 18. Where a student is below 18, the Institute is responsible for obtaining verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before the student uses Dhyeodaya. We do not track children for advertising or build behavioural profiles for any purpose other than the student’s own learning and exams.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see the data held about you, correct or update it, erase it (delete your account), withdraw consent, nominate a person to act for you, and complain.

  • Students: please contact your Institute first; it controls your account. You may also write to us (section 9) and we will pass the request to your Institute and help it respond.
  • To verify it is you we will ask for your registered mobile number and the name of your Institute.
  • We acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them as soon as possible, and in any case within 30 days.
  • If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Data stored on your device

The app stores a few small settings in your browser’s local storage, for example your Institute’s join code, text size in exams, a paused mock test, and which app version you have seen. These stay on your device, are not used for tracking, and you can clear them from your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
